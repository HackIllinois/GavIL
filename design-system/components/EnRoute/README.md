# EnRoute

The walk to the next table: the hero signpost naming the table and its room, "Look for" the project on its last board, the team, and after a few minutes "Can’t find it?" with the floor plan and staff.

- **Provide:** `table` (the placard code, `A12`), `room` (`4217`), `label` (the board's text, e.g. "Room 4217 · Siebel Center, 4th floor"), `hint`, `project` `{name, team}` (team as names or a comma list; first names are shown), `onMap` (or `mapHref`), `lost` (true once the judge has walked for `dur-cant-find`, 3 min), `onAskStaff`, optional `children` (rendered under the look-for lines; the app puts the memo card for the table just left outside the first screen instead: see below).
- The visit has two phases: **en route** (this) and **at the table** (`TableCheck`). The parent shows the timer, a `TableProgress` plaque ("Next table · 1 of ~4": the total is an estimate, see `Wayfinding`), **Skip** and **I’m at the table** in its action bar; nothing here can be judged yet.
- **The first screen and the memo cue.** The plaque, the cue and this signpost are one block, the first screen. When a memo card for the table just left follows it (`TableTalk`, mode `memo`, under an eyebrow "Memo · NeuralMesh"), that block is at least as tall as the screen under the top band (`100svh` less `--gv-top-h` and the body's padding and gap), so the card starts below it and is never half under the action bar at rest. One line between the plaque and the signpost, a secondary 56px button with a mic and a chevron ("Memo · NeuralMesh"), takes the judge to the card (scroll, then focus inside it, without scrolling again), and a status region that is already on the screen says "A memo about NeuralMesh is below." once. With no memo there is no cue, no card and no minimum height.
- The hero is `Wayfinding size="hero"`: the signpost's big arrow says "TABLE A12", and the last board says "Look for" and the project's name, so who to find is on the sign itself. Under it, ledes name the team's first names (so the judge can ask before the check), say which way the numbers run, and tell the judge what to bring to the placard: scan its QR, or type the table code and the 2 characters after the dot.
- After `dur-cant-find` the parent sets `lost`: a warn alert offers the floor plan and **Ask staff** (the can’t-find ping). It enters once (rise, an opacity fade under reduced motion). The words are said once by a polite live region that holds only the text (never the buttons), then cleared, so reading the page line by line doesn’t hear them twice.
