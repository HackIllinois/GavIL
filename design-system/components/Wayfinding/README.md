# Wayfinding

Answers "where do I go?" on every judge screen. The inline sign is an arrow plank with the table code on one line; the hero is the 2027 signpost lettered with the room or table.

- **Provide:** `room`, optional `table` (a room-qualified code like `A12`, as printed on the placard), `label`, `hint`, `noun` (hero, default "Room"), `size='hero'`, `board` + `boardLabel` (hero: the last board, e.g. "Look for" / "NeuralMesh"; blank without it), `mapHref`/`onMap`, `mapLabel` (false hides the map link), `artState` (`loading`|`ready`|`failed`: hero only, normally decided by the picture itself; set it to preview a state). With neither `mapHref` nor `onMap` there is no map control at all: never a dead link. With both, a plain tap runs `onMap` (the in-app floor plan) and a modified click (new tab) follows `mapHref`.
- **Inline** is the arrow plank `art-plank-point-right` (its tip points right, where to go) as a 3-slice, so its notch and tip never smear: the value is "Table A12" in `--type-title`, never split across lines (it ellipsizes before it wraps); the room rides in the label ("Room 4217 · 1 of 4"). Cream on the plank over the `plank-scrim` veil, at least 4.8:1 on every pixel under the letters.
- **Hero** is the blank signpost `art-signpost` (718 × 1135, drawn `sign-w` = 280px wide) lettered with live text, never a picture of text: the label on the top board, the noun ("ROOM", "TABLE") on the first arrow, the value ("4217", "A12") on the big arrow in `--type-sign`, the floor plan on the second arrow (a 48px+ control), `board` on the last board, and the hint as a lede under it. The signs are light tan, so the lettering is `ink-sign` (dark) over the `sign-scrim` glow, at least 5.5:1 on every pixel under the letters; each text is rotated to its sign. A bare number is never mistaken for a time or a code: the noun always comes with it.
- **The picture is not guaranteed** (a loud room, flaky Wi-Fi): `data-art` says `ready`, `loading` or `failed`. Until the art is decoded (hidden for `dur-delay` first, so a fast load never flashes) and when it fails, the five boxes are flat wood plates with cream lettering (at least 4.5:1, same places and rotation), the broken-picture box is hidden and keeps its room. Dark ink on the painted ground never shows.
- **It fits what is written:** the label takes two lines, the noun and the value one line shrunk to fit the sign (`sign-glyph`, `sign-fit-min`), a value past `sign-fit-chars` (12) two lines at `title-sm`, then an ellipsis. Text is whole in the DOM and clipped by its box: nothing spills over the next sign or the post.
- Show the inline form above every project so judges never hunt for a table.
