# ConsentSheet

Spoken consent before recording: the judge reads a script naming who processes the audio and what each gets; "Everyone agreed · start" is the only way the microphone opens.

- **Provide:** `onConsent()` (start: the parent starts the engine only now, **inside this tap**: iOS opens the microphone only from a gesture, so it runs before the sheet’s exit animation), `onDecline()` ("Not this time": record nothing, store no reason), `onCancel()` (Escape, scrim or drag: the sheet just closes, nothing is declined; required), `onClosed()` (after the exit that follows a yes), `ai` (`false` = AI summaries off), optional `script`, `title`.
- A `Sheet`: `role=dialog`, `aria-modal`, focus trapped, everything behind inert, focus returns to **Ask and record**.
- The words come from one config, `GavIL.talk.copy({ai})`, in two variants. **AI summaries on** (the default) is character for character web/lib/talk/policy.ts's `CONSENT_SCRIPT` and `TEAM_NOTICE` (`scripts/sync_policy.py` writes it into the bundle and the suite compares the two; the AI-off variant, the lists below and the retention line are preview-only until policy.ts has them): Cloudflare transcribes, Anthropic summarizes, the audio isn’t kept, only the judge sees the notes. **Off** drops Anthropic: Cloudflare transcribes and the judge’s phone picks out the highlights.
- The script is in `--type-read-aloud` (20px). Under it, exactly what each processor gets: Cloudflare gets the audio (deleted once it is transcribed); Anthropic gets the transcript, the judge’s typed notes, the project’s name, tagline and sponsor tracks, and the team’s first names (no full names or emails), or nothing when AI summaries are off. Then: only you see the notes, nobody is identified by voice (no diarization or voiceprints: BIPA). **What the placard tells the team** opens the notice printed on the placard (`copy().notice`). A retention line (`copy().retention`: transcripts are deleted 7 days after the event) sits under the notice, as it does on the placard. The footnote coaches distance and repeats that anyone can say no.
- The first choice wins: the sheet latches "Everyone agreed", "Not this time" or a cancel before it runs any callback, so a decline that is still leaving can never be reversed by a late tap, and a yes can never be followed by a decline or a cancel. A dismissal counts as a choice from the moment it starts (`close.closing()`): a yes tapped while Escape, the scrim or a drag is still closing the sheet is ignored, so the microphone never opens for a sheet the judge was already closing.
- Off by default per event (Admin → Settings) until counsel signs off (Illinois Eavesdropping Act, BIPA).
