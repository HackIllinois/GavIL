# Sheet

A bottom-sheet dialog. It slides up on the drawer curve, leaves fast, scrolls inside and rides above the iPhone keyboard; the rope handle drags it down.

- **Provide:** `title`, optional `titleId`, `lede`, `onClose` (required: Escape, the scrim and a drag are the way out, and a sheet that can't be dismissed would trap a keyboard or screen-reader judge; without it the sheet warns in the console and renders nothing: it never makes the page inert and never shows a sheet nothing can dismiss), `children`, `actions`, `initialFocus()` (default: the sheet's title, the static element at its top as APG allows, so the judge hears what the sheet is, no keyboard pops up over a text field and a long sheet never opens scrolled to its bottom action; Tab then goes to the first control and Shift+Tab wraps to the last). `children` and `actions` may be functions of `close(then)`: close plays the exit, then calls `then`; `close.closing()` is true as soon as any way out has started (a button, Escape, the scrim or a drag), so a button tapped during the exit can ignore itself.
- Dialog semantics: `role=dialog` + `aria-modal`, everything outside is `inert` while it is open, focus moves in and is trapped (hidden and inert controls are skipped), Escape / scrim / drag-down close it, focus returns to the opener. Unmounting mid-exit cancels the pending callback; a lost or cancelled pointer snaps it back.
- Double-tap safe: when a finger closes it (a button, the scrim, a drag), a clear shield (`GavIL.tapShield()`) takes taps for `dur-exit` + `dur-settle` from the moment it starts to close, so the second tap on "Close map" or "Back" never lands on Submit or Skip underneath. The button the finger pressed also keeps its own footprint shielded until `dur-retap` (1.5 s): the consent sheet's "Everyone agreed · start" sits exactly over Submit, and a judge who thinks nothing happened taps again after a second. Only that spot is held back. Keys are never blocked.
- Enter `dur-sheet` (300ms) on `ease-drawer`; exit `dur-exit` (120ms); a fast flick dismisses even if short. Under reduced motion it fades instead of sliding, and does not follow the finger while dragged (release still closes it).
- `max-height` with internal scroll and `overscroll-behavior: contain`; the actions stay pinned at the bottom.
